# Retail Sales Analysis (2022-2024)

An exploratory analysis of a retail dataset covering three years of orders, customers, products and stores. The goal was to find out which categories and sub-categories drive sales and which ones actually drive profit.

## Questions I answered
- Which categories make the most sales and profit?
- Which sub-categories are the most and least profitable?
- Is there a sub-category with high sales but a low margin?

## Key findings
- Technology has the most sales (about 1.31M) but Office Supplies is the most profitable category (about 348K profit, ~40% margin).
- Accessories is the strongest sub-category, with the highest sales and profit.
- Printers has the 2nd highest sales but a margin of only 18%.
